Privacy Notice

Version {{ version }}. Questions: {{ contact }}

What we store
  • Your account: username, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Projects: project details, who is on the team and their roles.
  • Your documents: files you upload, the text we read from them, and the results of analysing them (tasks, owners, dates, decisions, questions and answers, transcripts).
  • Handovers: each approved version, who approved it, who downloaded it and who confirmed receipt.
  • Activity log: who did what and when, to keep decisions accountable.
How AI is used

To analyse a project, we send document text to a third-party AI service (Google Gemini). Photos, scans and audio are sent as files to be turned into text, and they cannot be cleaned first. We try to hide passwords and keys in text before it is sent, but this cannot be guaranteed. Files marked restricted are never sent to the AI. The AI provider handles data under its own terms, which we do not control. Do not upload anything you are not allowed to share with a third-party AI service. AI results can be wrong, so people review and approve everything before it becomes a handover.

Who can see your data

Only people on the project, and only what their role and each file's access level allow. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

A project and all its files are deleted 90 days after the latest handover is built by default. We email the managers 14 days before. Managers can extend this, or delete a project immediately. Deleting removes the files and analysed data. The activity log keeps a bare record (time, action and person) with its details removed.

Your choices
  • Managers can delete a project at any time from its Team page.
  • To close your account or ask about your data, email {{ contact }}.
Security

We use hashed passwords, login attempt limits, permission checks on every request, protected file access, upload checks and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes

If this notice changes in a meaningful way, we will update the version above and tell you.
